## Research and Scholarly Projects

### Dissertation Research  
**Generative AI in Graduate Education: Master’s and Doctoral Students’ Experiences and Academic Practices**  
University of Idaho | 2026  

- Explored graduate students’ lived experiences using generative AI in academic work.
- Examined how students use AI for brainstorming, summarizing, drafting, language refinement, and learning support.
- Investigated ethical concerns related to accuracy, privacy, academic integrity, institutional expectations, and overreliance.
- Used qualitative phenomenological methods, semi-structured interviews, and reflexive thematic analysis.

### High-Impact Online Professional Learning Experiences for Teachers to Support Accelerated Learning for Students (CALM)  
University of Idaho  

- Contributed to a professional learning project designed to support teachers through online professional development.
- Focused on research-based strategies, interactive tools, collaboration, and support for student learning.
- Supported qualitative data analysis by reviewing and refining AI-generated interview transcripts using Otter.ai, cleaning and organizing textual data, and preparing transcripts for thematic coding and interpretation.
